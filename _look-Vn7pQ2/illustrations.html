Empty-state illustrations

Three directions for the screen a patient sees on day one, before they have any photos. All drawn as SVG, so they stay sharp at any size, weigh almost nothing and take their colour from the palette — change the theme and they follow.

Option A · Sprout
Recovery, growing
Quiet optimism without saying anything twee. Reads as progress rather than medicine.
Option B · New day
One day at a time
Calm and neutral. The safest of the three, and the least likely to date.
Option C · Held
Looked after
Two forms, one supporting the other. Warmest, but the most abstract — it needs the caption to land.
My pick: A. It says the one thing a patient on day one most needs to hear, which is that this gets better, and it does it without a face, a cartoon or a motivational slogan. B is the safe fallback. C is lovely but leans on its caption.
Nothing here is live. Say which and it goes on the empty diary screen.
